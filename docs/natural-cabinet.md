# Natural cabinet, 2.6-school

The miniature now uses a camera-based perspective cuboid. Each source room has a camera principal point and depth/distance ratio. Camera coordinates use the same source-image crop matrix as the background and the actual safe-area-clamped cabinet envelope. The rear rectangle scales toward that vanishing point, while the front remains parallel to the wall. A solved uniform front scale fits the face union while holding the cropped room camera fixed. Rear corners are computed directly from that final front and camera, so no subsequent normalization can move the vanishing point. The front retains its proportions, horizontal centering, and feet at the envelope bottom. Only physically visible side and top faces draw. Rear uprights shorten, shared crown edges coincide, and front feet stay grounded.

Each finish loads a bounded, cached square wood surface, with cabinet bitmap fallback. The body ends at the plinth above the sprite feet. The drawable color filter avoids a full-size offscreen layer; zero projection clears the filter. Ground-shadow geometry uses explicit layout dimensions before the first miniature layout. A room-specific color matrix blends with the projection animation, adjusting the complete miniature including books without changing the expanded UI. Narrow foot contacts follow the sprite feet at 5% and 95% of the projected front span, including the shadow envelope offset. The soft cast direction follows the room window/light location independently of the camera. All animation timing, gestures, data and theme independence remain unchanged.

Pure JDK geometry tests cover all rooms, portrait/landscape/small windows, actual crop transforms, identity endpoints, floor contact, shared crown edges, bounds, visibility and rear height ratios. Native tests compare actual rendered matrices and accept legitimately hidden faces. Screenshot capture now requires a fresh pre-draw frame and checks settled collapsed visibility. Native device execution and visual calibration remain CI responsibilities.

Train front feet use source y=.74 with height=.23: the source parquet starts around .713, and the rear floor projection approaches that boundary instead of anchoring the cabinet on the wooden baseboard. Other room anchors remain unchanged.
